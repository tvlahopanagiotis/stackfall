          Controls

            Touch controls

            Gestures hide the pad so the well gets the room. On the well: drag sideways to move, drag down to soft drop, tap the left or right half to rotate that way, flick down to hard drop. Flick up, tap with two fingers or tap the Hold slip to hold. Pause sits at the top right.

            Handling

            Keys

            Fixed keys: Enter starts, M sound, N music, G ghost.

            Gamepad: D-pad or left stick moves, Up hard drops, A rotates right, B rotates left, X or LB holds, Start pauses, Back banks a Run round.
